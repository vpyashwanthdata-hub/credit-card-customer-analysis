# 💳 Credit Card Customer Analysis

> A comprehensive **Data Cleaning** and **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** project on the **BankChurners (Credit Card Customer)** dataset using Python. This project explores customer demographics, spending behavior, credit utilization, transaction activity, and customer attrition patterns to generate meaningful business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer retention is one of the most important challenges in the banking industry. Understanding customer behavior and identifying the factors associated with customer attrition can help organizations improve retention strategies and increase long-term profitability.

This project performs an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the BankChurners dataset by:

- Assessing data quality
- Cleaning the dataset
- Exploring customer demographics
- Analyzing spending and credit behavior
- Investigating customer attrition
- Discovering feature relationships
- Generating business recommendations

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Assess the quality of the dataset.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Understand customer demographics.
- Analyze spending and transaction behavior.
- Explore credit utilization patterns.
- Identify factors associated with customer attrition.
- Discover relationships among numerical features.
- Generate business insights and recommendations.

# 📈 Key Findings

- Approximately **84%** of customers remain active, while **16%** have attrited.
- Most customers are between **40–55 years** of age.
- Female customers slightly outnumber male customers.
- Graduate customers represent the largest educational group.
- Most customers belong to the **Less than $40K** income category.
- Most customers maintain relatively low credit utilization.
- Lower transaction activity is associated with higher attrition.
- Customer churn is influenced by multiple behavioral factors rather than a single feature.
